Track to Progress

1880s Canadian Railway Simulation

🚂 The Mission

You and your team are running an 1880s railway company. Your goal is to build a train line all the way across Canada from Montreal to Vancouver (100 spaces).

⚠️ The Catch: How to Win

Getting to Vancouver first doesn't automatically make you the winner! The real winner reaches B.C. with the Least Amount of Bank Debt and the Least Amount of Harm (Human Rights & Environmental).

🎲 Rules of Engagement

  • The Rule of 6: You must have all 6 unique resources to move on the main Red Line.
  • Pay the Price: After moving on the Red Line, you must discard 1 resource.
  • Rebuild: If you are missing a resource, you cannot roll on the Red Line. You must either pay $2M for a blind draw, or take a Detour (half speed, but picks up resources).
