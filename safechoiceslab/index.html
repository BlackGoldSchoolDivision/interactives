Proven Peer Refusal Tactics (C.L.E.A.R)

1. The Direct & Firm "No"

"No thanks, that's not for me." Keep posture relaxed, tone calm, and make steady eye contact without hostility.

2. The Plausible Excuse or Exit Call

"I have sports practice early tomorrow, need my lungs." Or text a pre-arranged emergency code word to a guardian to get an immediate phone call home.

3. The Better Alternative

"I'm not doing that. Let's go grab smoothies or hit the basketball court instead." Pivot the group to something constructive.

4. The Broken Record

Repeat the same simple refusal calmly without getting defensive: "Nah, I'm good." "Still nah." Eventually the pressure dissipates.

5. Body Language & Walking Away

If peers don't respect your boundaries, physically walking away is strength, not weakness. Reconnect with a safer group.
