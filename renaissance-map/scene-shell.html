About this illustrated atlas

This scene lets you try three mapping questions before the artwork is carried into the full assignment.

Coastlines and rivers use Natural Earth 1:50m data. The historical boundaries use André Ourednik’s circa-1400 reconstruction, with the same classroom adaptations as the full Renaissance activity. Historical borders remain approximate.

The paper, blue wash, ship and mountain illustrations are decorative artwork. The engraved mountains indicate broad mountain areas; they are not an elevation model.
