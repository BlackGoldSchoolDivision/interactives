Access settings

LIAM mode: two large answer buttons, one step at a time, a still map, and a separate Next button. Every mapping task stays available. Use ChromeOS Face Control or your usual pointer to click; this activity does not use the camera.

Hold-to-select is optional. Leave it off if your face-control tool already generates clicks. There is no timer or penalty for trying again.

About the maps

Europe’s outlines are digitised from the late-medieval outline map in your classroom assignment. Italian state outlines are simplified approximations; Venice’s overseas possessions are not shown. Castile is used for the assignment’s “Spain” label. These are historical teaching maps, not modern borders.

The separate Ottoman map is digitised from the assignment’s reference showing its extent in 1683. Its ruling religion was Islam, but the empire contained many religious communities.

Yellow, red and green follow the assignment’s teaching colour key. The Renaissance spread over centuries through many routes; the arrows show classroom connections rather than dated journeys. Spain is grouped with the northern destinations as instructed in the assignment.

Sources

Classroom source: Interactive Renaissance Map, including the original questions and textbook page references. Background reading: The Met: Florence and Central Italy; The Met: Venice and trade; The Met: the Northern Renaissance.
