# Literal Chaos artwork

Twelve original generated raster sprites created for this project on 2026-10-24 using the built-in image-generation tool, then isolated, visually checked, and compressed to transparent WebP for local delivery. No external image service is needed by the game. Generated artwork is not a historical source or a scientific diagram.

Files: `bowl.webp`, `beans.webp`, `cake.webp`, `ice.webp`, `butterfly.webp`, `cloud.webp`, `heart.webp`, `key.webp`, `flame.webp`, `moon.webp`, `clock.webp`, `feather.webp`.

Each image has genuine alpha transparency and at least an 8-pixel transparent outer margin. Shapes are approximately 260–350 pixels wide, suitable for their intended layered game use. The total sprite download is under 210 KB. The beans cluster contains five disconnected beans; the cloud retains its separate raindrops. Small adjacent-cell specks were removed during sprite extraction.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one transparent game sprite atlas, 3 columns by 4 rows, twelve isolated objects for a flat illustrated classroom game "Literal Chaos". Precise row-major layout, each object centred wholly within its cell with very generous empty transparent gutters, no object crossing a cell boundary. Equal cells. No labels, no text, no numbers, no grid lines, no frame. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT alpha background across the whole atlas, not checkerboard artwork. Flat screen-print cutout style, bold dark navy slightly imperfect outlines, solid tomato red, sky blue, warm cream, mustard accents, tiny restrained ink grain, no soft shadows, no 3D, no airbrush, no faces, no hands. Clear large silhouette read at small sizes.
> Row 1: (1) large tomato red mixing bowl tipped diagonally on its side, empty inside, opening visible toward upper right; (2) loose cluster of five cream and dark-red kidney beans, separated shapes with small transparent gaps; (3) triangular yellow cake slice with white icing and tiny red cherry.
> Row 2: (4) angular sky-blue ice cube with dark outline and a few flat white highlight facets; (5) small butterfly with skyblue and tomato-red wings, symmetrical spread wings; (6) sky-blue storm cloud with three hanging dark raindrops.
> Row 3: (7) simple hand-printed red heart; (8) old-fashioned mustard gold key with single round bow and two teeth; (9) small red and orange flame, clear contained silhouette.
> Row 4: (10) warm cream crescent paper moon; (11) small round skyblue alarm clock with white face, two hands and simple tick marks but NO numbers; (12) long soft cream feather with a dark navy central quill.
> The objects are movable layered game art, not an illustration scene. Keep all twelve objects independent with no surrounding props. Composition must have obvious 3x4 alignment for deterministic cropping. Canvas portrait 3:4 ratio.

Raw atlas and QA montage are intermediate working files, not application dependencies.
