Learning Lab ↗

Go beyond naming the device

Decode uses 36 original passages across three flexible difficulty levels. Rounds contain up to six expressions without repeats. A correct first attempt earns 100 points; a correct retry earns 50. Difficulty reflects language demands, not a grade placement test.

Comic Lab is open-ended. Students create a literal panel and a figurative context, paraphrase the meaning, and explain what the expression adds. Their writing is not automatically graded.

  • Idioms: established expressions with a conventional meaning that goes beyond the individual words.
  • Similes: explicit comparisons, often using “like” or “as”.
  • Metaphors: descriptions of one thing in terms of another.
  • Hyperbole: deliberate exaggeration for effect.
  • Personification: giving a nonhuman thing human qualities, intentions, or actions.

Categories can overlap. Ask students to defend another reasonable label with evidence. Some pictures exaggerate individual words; they are playful starting points, not historical explanations of an expression.

Try revising a passage so a wrong answer becomes reasonable, or compare a figurative expression with a plain version. Discuss what changed. Suggested Grades 2–6+, with adult support for younger readers.
