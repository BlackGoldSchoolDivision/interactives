How to Save & Load Your Game

This tool runs entirely in your web browser. This is great for privacy, but it means there is no central database to automatically save your prompts.

Saving (Exporting Data)

When you finish creating prompts, click "Export Data (.json)". Think of a .json file just like a .docx (Word) or .pptx (PowerPoint) file—it's simply a save file for your game data.

  • It will download to your computer's Downloads folder.
  • You can rename this file (e.g., biology-quiz.json) to keep it organized.

Loading (Importing Data)

To play the game later, or to edit your prompts, open this website again and click "Import Data".

  • Select the .json file you previously downloaded.
  • Your prompts will instantly appear in the list, ready to play or edit!

Sharing with colleagues?

You can email your saved .json file to other teachers. They can open this site, click "Import Data", select the file, and use your exact quiz!
