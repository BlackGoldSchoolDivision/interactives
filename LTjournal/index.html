Chapter 1: The Ripple Effect of Change

As we grow, life transitions (like starting high school, moving, or joining a new team) act like a stone dropped in a pond. They create ripples that affect our relationships.

Think of a general example for each of the three impacts below:

🌧️ 1. Present Challenges

Sometimes changes create distance or conflict in relationships.

🌱 2. Strengthen Existing Relationships

Going through changes together can bring people closer.

✨ 3. Opportunities for New Relationships

New environments introduce us to new people.
