Question of the Week

Add the three photos once, then move through all 33 choice slides. Names, photos, and answers are saved on this device.

Tip: drag a choice card, or tap a choice and then tap a student card.

How To Use

  1. Add 2 student pictures and a picture of the target student.
  2. Choose the Question of the Week.
  3. Have the target student find the pictured peers and ask the question.
  4. This can be done using an AAC device or EA support.
  5. Drag or tap the selected choice onto the picture of the student who answered.
  6. Use the selected choice as the subject of a predictable sentence if you want to extend the activity.

Touchscreen: tap a choice, then tap a student card. Mouse users can drag choices directly.
