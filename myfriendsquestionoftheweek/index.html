Question of the Week

Set up people once. Their name cards or photos are reused for every question and saved on this device.

People Cards

Type a name for a reusable card, or add a photo. Photos appear as a small face/avatar so portrait pictures do not get stretched or heavily cropped. You can switch back to name-only anytime.
Tip: type names first. Photos are optional.

How To Use

  1. In People Cards, type the two peer names and the target student's name.
  2. If you have photos, choose Add photo. If not, the typed name automatically becomes a reusable name card.
  3. Choose the Question of the Week.
  4. Have the target student ask the pictured peer the question.
  5. Drag the selected choice onto that person's card, or tap the choice and then tap the person's card.
  6. The people cards, photos, names, answers, and current question stay saved on this device.

Both options work: a person can use a photo card or a name-only card. Choose “Name only” at any time to remove the photo but keep the name.
