Q What is a Rational Number?

A rational number is any number that can be expressed as the quotient or fraction a/b, where a and b are integers, and b is not equal to zero. Because they are fractions, all rational numbers can be written as decimals that either terminate (end) or repeat in a pattern.

Subsets of Rational Numbers

  • Q
    Rational Numbers: Fractions and decimals that fall between integers (like 1/2, -0.75, 0.333...) as well as all sets below.
  • Z
    Integers: Whole numbers and their negative counterparts (..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2...).
  • W
    Whole Numbers: The natural numbers, plus the number zero (0, 1, 2, 3...).
  • N
    Natural Numbers: Positive counting numbers starting from one (1, 2, 3, 4...).
