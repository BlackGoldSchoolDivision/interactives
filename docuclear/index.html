What's your Brain Style?

Six Seconds Emotional Intelligence Network

Your Brain Style is a snapshot of how your brain processes cognitive and emotional data. It provides insight into what you tend to notice and feel motivated by - and why.

Your Brain Style is based on three elements:

  1. Focus: Do you prefer data that is analytical or emotional?
  2. Decisions: Do you tend to protect or innovate?
  3. Drive: Are you usually motivated by the practical or the idealistic?

Image Description: A circular chart showing the 8 brain styles

There are 8 primary Brain Styles:

  • Sage: Caring, Protective, Careful.
  • Energizer: Committed, Creative, Lofty.
  • Guardian: Protective, Careful, Pragmatic.
  • Visionary: Creative, Real-world, Long term.
  • Deliverer: Caring, Passionate, Task-oriented.
  • Strategist: Precise, Analytical, Future-oriented.
  • Inventor: Accurate, Transformative, Open.
  • Scientist: Generative, Careful, Precise.
