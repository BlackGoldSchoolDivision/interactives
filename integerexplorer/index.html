Addition & Subtraction

Same Signs: Add & Keep Sign

Add the absolute values together and keep the common sign.

3 + 5 = 8
-3 + (-5) = -8
Different Signs: Subtract & Keep Larger

Subtract the smaller number from the larger number. Take the sign of the number with the larger absolute value.

-7 + 4 = -3  (7 is bigger, answer is negative)
8 + (-2) = 6   (8 is bigger, answer is positive)
Subtracting a Negative?

Two minuses side-by-side turn into a positive! (Add the opposite)

5 - (-3)  ➔  5 + 3 = 8

Multiplication & Division

Same Signs = Positive Answer

Multiplying or dividing two numbers with the SAME sign always gives a POSITIVE result.

(+4) × (+3) = +12
(-4) × (-3) = +12
(-10) ÷ (-2) = +5
Different Signs = Negative Answer

Multiplying or dividing two numbers with DIFFERENT signs always gives a NEGATIVE result.

(+4) × (-3) = -12
(-4) × (+3) = -12
(-10) ÷ (+2) = -5
