Rules & examples

Use the visual models first. These rules explain what you notice.

Adding integers

−1−1+1+1+1

Opposite chips make zero pairs. Count what's left.

−2 + 3 = 1

Subtracting integers

−1+1

You can add zero pairs to have enough chips to take away. The total stays the same.

3 − (−2) = 5

Multiplying integers

−1−1−1

Think about equal groups. Same signs produce a positive product; different signs produce a negative product.

−3 × 4 = −12

Dividing integers

+1+1+1+1

Think of equal groups and the sign of the quotient. Dividing by zero is undefined.

−8 ÷ (−2) = 4
