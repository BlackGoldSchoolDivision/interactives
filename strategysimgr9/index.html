Strategy Playbook

Your toolkit for handling peer pressure, making safe decisions, and setting boundaries.

1. Direct Refusal

Saying "No" clearly and firmly without apologizing. Keep body language confident.

2. Broken Record

Repeating your refusal over and over without engaging in an argument. "No thanks. Like I said, no thanks."

3. Blame the Rules/Adults

Using consequences as a shield. "My coach will bench me," or "My parents check my location."

4. Give a Fact/Reason

Explaining why based on health or safety. "I have asthma," or "That stuff causes anxiety."

5. Distraction / Humor

Changing the subject or making a joke to defuse the tension and move the conversation away.

6. The Exit Strategy

Physically leaving the situation. "I gotta go to the bathroom," or "My mom is outside."

7. Involving a Trusted Adult

Crucial for Emergencies: When someone's physical safety is at immediate risk (medical distress, impaired driving, severe bullying), secrecy is no longer an option. You must contact a parent, teacher, counselor, or 911 immediately.
