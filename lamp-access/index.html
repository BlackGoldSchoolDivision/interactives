Chromebook Face Control

  1. Open Chromebook Settings → Accessibility → Cursor and touchpad → Face control.
  2. Turn on head movement for the pointer. Assign a comfortable facial gesture to Left-click.
  3. Move to a button and use the click gesture. Choose Full screen to make the grid larger.

These are ordinary buttons, so ChromeOS Face Control and Automatic clicks can activate them. The website does not use your camera. Choose one click method at a time to avoid duplicate selections.

All pages keep the same 12 × 7 grid. A page button opens vocabulary; a word button adds speech. Some words use a second selection in the same position.

On a narrow screen, scroll the board sideways. Rows and columns stay in their original order.

About this trial

A browser communication board for testing access on a Chromebook. The included reference pages preserve the button positions and symbols in PRC-Saltillo’s published low-tech LAMP Words for Life boards. This is an independent school prototype, not the official LAMP Words for Life application or its complete vocabulary engine.

Compare the paths you plan to practise with the student’s own vocabulary file. Personalised files and newer versions may differ from this 2019 reference set.

Messages stay in this tab and are cleared when it closes. Only voice preferences are saved on this device. Speech uses the device’s browser voices; some voices may need a connection.
