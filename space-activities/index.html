A few ways to explore

Choose an activity from the menu. Read the two passages when you need evidence.

  • Move cards: drag a card to a box. On a touch screen, tap a card, then tap Place here.
  • Use a keyboard: press Enter or Space to select a card, Tab to a Place here button, then Enter to move it.
  • Keep thinking: check the card sorts, then explain your ideas in your own words. Writing is for discussion with your teacher.
  • Keep your work: work saves in this browser when storage is available. Save work downloads a file you can keep or give to your teacher. Load work opens it.

On a shared device, save your file and use New learner before the next student. Teacher view shows teaching notes and model responses; it is a view switch, with no account or password.
