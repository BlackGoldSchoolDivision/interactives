Phase 2: Exponent Laws Review

Let's quickly review the rules before we practice!

Product Law

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents.

xa · xb = xa+b

Quotient Law

When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the exponents.

xa ÷ xb = xa-b

Power of a Power Law

When raising a power to another power, multiply the exponents.

(xa)b = xa · b

Zero Exponent Law

Any non-zero base raised to the power of zero is always one.

x0 = 1
