BUILD THE HABITS BEFORE THE BIG DISCUSSION

Teach one discussion move at a time

Model it, rehearse it in pairs, use it in an activity, then reflect on its effect. These are original classroom practice suggestions.

Agree on the conditions for dialogue

Listen to understand. Give reasons. Challenge ideas respectfully. Leave room for others. Allow thinking time, a pass, and supported ways to contribute. Choose material students can discuss without requiring personal disclosure.

A five-minute practice

  1. Pick one move and model it with a familiar example.
  2. Partners practise: one shares an idea, the other uses the move. Swap roles.
  3. Ask: “What did that move help us understand?” Carry it into the next discussion.
