# Morpheme Madness

A student-facing adaptation of Shelley Blackwell's June 2021 Morpheme Madness student workbook, instructor guide, and accompanying movement activities. The original student workbook is included for classroom reference.

Open index.html through a static web server. Play five meaning-clue puzzles in each of 23 lessons or select Student workbook in the Lessons menu. Word sorts, sums, builds, matrices, family trees, and open responses follow the student pages. The audio button reads the current clue or completed word. Native browser voices are preferred, with recorded Flite clips as a fallback. Progress is stored on the current device.

The digital activities distinguish spelling look-alikes and allow written definitions and creative words. The print source is credited to its author. Bricolage Grotesque is distributed under the SIL Open Font License in assets/OFL.txt. The illustrations are original generated artwork.
