Templates

The five built-in activity models used by Science Inquiry Builder v1.0.

🧪 Inquiry Lab

Inquiry Lab

Use for outcomes that lend themselves to hands-on investigation, variables, observations, data tables, graphing, and conclusions.

  • Essential question
  • Prediction or hypothesis
  • Variables and materials
  • Procedure and data table
  • Analysis and conclusion
⚙️ Design Challenge

Design Challenge

Use for systems, structures, mechanical design, technologies, and prototype building tasks.

  • Problem
  • Criteria and constraints
  • Plan and sketch
  • Test data
  • Troubleshooting and redesign
💡 Evidence & Decision

Evidence & Decision

Use when students need to study evidence, weigh trade-offs, and justify an environmental or scientific decision.

  • Issue or scenario
  • What we need to know
  • Evidence table
  • Benefits and trade-offs
  • Claim and justification
🔎 Investigative Questions

Investigative Questions

Generate strong, testable, or open-ended science questions connected to the selected outcome.

  • Question stems
  • What to change or compare
  • What to measure or observe
  • Suggested evidence collection
📊 Review & Retrieval

Review & Retrieval

Produce a balanced review set with recall, explain, apply, analyze, and transfer prompts.

  • Recall questions
  • Explain why/how
  • Application questions
  • Analyze data or scenarios
  • Transfer prompt
