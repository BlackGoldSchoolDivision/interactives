A language playground

Students test how verbs, setting, and descriptive language influence a reader’s interpretation. Suggested for Grades 3–6 and beyond; read aloud and discuss together with younger students.

  • Make a prediction before changing a word. Compare it with what the simulation does.
  • Keep the subject and setting constant. Change one word and defend the effect.
  • Challenge the model: “I would picture this differently because…”
  • Describe the same place through the eyes of two different narrators.
  • Use the two scenes as a starting point for writing a paragraph in each voice.

The sound and scene are deliberately authored responses to a curated word bank. They do not assess writing or determine a correct emotional response. “Keep this pair” records the student’s experiment, without grading it.
