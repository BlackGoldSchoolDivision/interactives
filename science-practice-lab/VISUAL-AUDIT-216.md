# Science PAT Practice · Visual audit of all 216 questions

**Purpose:** Track visual presentation improvements without breaking Grade 6 scoring, answers or curricular claims. Image presence is not a claim of visual excellence. Real photos, original generated illustrations and accurately coded scientific diagrams have different purposes. Numerical questions can legitimately use tables rather than photographs.

## Release gates

New visuals need factual and source checks, alt descriptions that do not state answers, 320 px touchscreen placement, image-load verification, browser scoring, and a curriculum-relevant student purpose. Keep the public app free of teacher-only answer browsers. Keep actual numbers, force directions and other measured data as independently validated code/data.
