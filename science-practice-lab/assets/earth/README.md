# Earth Systems asset bank

**Status: planned.** No generated Earth Systems artwork has been committed here yet. The first requests are in [asset-plan.json](../asset-plan.json). Future originals may show weather phenomena, climate-action contexts, storm-forecast tools, communities' changing environments and geographic settings.

Illustrative satellite-like hurricane graphics are never labelled as measured satellite records. Climate values and geographical measurements come from trusted data or deliberate classroom examples, not the generative image. Do not invent or claim local First Nations, Métis or Inuit teachings or observations.
