📜 Track to Progress — Field Manual

🎯 Mission: Run an 1880s railway company from Montreal to Vancouver. To win, reach BC with the least Bank Debt and least Human Rights & Environmental Harm.

🎒 Starting Gear: $10 Million Cash Purse + 6 unique Resource Cards (Wood, Ore, Coal, Water, Engine, Labour).

🎲 Turn Execution:
1. On the Main Red Line, you must hold all 6 unique resources to roll 1 die (1–6) and advance.
2. Click a glowing space on the map to move your locomotive there.
3. After moving on the Main Red Line, discard 1 resource card from your hand.
4. If you land on a Circle (⭕) or X (✖) space, draw a Progress Card and resolve its historical dilemma.

🔄 Rebuilding Your Hand:
• Option A (Pay $2M): Pay $2M from your Cash Purse (or automatic Bank Loan if cash is empty) to draw the top card from the 20-card Resource Deck.
• Option B (Detour Loop): At any detour junction (Montreal, Shield East, Winnipeg, Calgary, Edmonton), click Detour Roll to turn off the Red Line without needing all 6 cards! You collect every free resource icon you pass over or land on along the loop.

📒 Ledger Modes: Switch anytime between Automatic Ledger and Manual Student Entry using the button in the top bar.
