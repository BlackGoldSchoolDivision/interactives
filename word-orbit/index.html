Word Orbit · Teacher notes

Build word families through meaningful prefixes and suffixes. familiar bases offer discoveries, from ponies and leaves to readability, independence and modernizing. Select vocabulary appropriate to your learners; the longer chains suit guided exploration.

A prefix joins before the current word, and a suffix joins after it. The centre shows the whole spelling and its meaningful parts. Each collected word has a short meaning, which can be heard with the centre button or revisited from the collection.

Try together: How does re- change play? What does -er tell us in player? Why does replaying still belong to the play family?

The activity uses a reviewed set of common word constructions, not an exhaustive dictionary. Only supported extensions and checked nonsense endings float into the play area. A real word outside the collection is not offered as a penalised match. New valid words earn 10 points, repeats earn 0, and decoys cost 3; scores may go below zero.

Spelling: The family keeps its base identity when its spelling changes. For example, care + ing → caring drops the final e. The game explains this change. Other notes explain changes such as jumpy → jumpier, readable → readability and flexible → flexibility. It does not teach every spelling rule.

Pause freezes the drift and the return-to-base animation. Device resizing preserves the current word, score and collection. There is no countdown, account, microphone, tracking or server-side student record. Progress lasts for the current page session. The downloadable copy works offline; speech depends on the device’s available voices.

Curriculum selection: The ELAL 1–6 spreadsheet names particular affixes, rather than prescribing these whole-word lists. Families were selected to practise its plural and tense endings, comparisons, prefixes, person-naming endings, adjective endings, and later derivations such as -ment, -ity, -ance, -ence, -en and -ize. Families can combine earlier and later word parts; choose examples suitable for your learners. This is focused practice, not complete coverage of every listed affix or learning outcome.

Instruction background: Reading Rockets: meaningful word parts. Word meanings here are original child-friendly paraphrases.
