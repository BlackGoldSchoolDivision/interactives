## Scope and safeguards

- Existing families remain available. The expansion adds box, jump, green, read, agree, act, art, hero, music, danger, assist, depend, dark, modern, pony, leaf and flex. There are 23 families and 213 target words in total.
- The content includes earlier and later patterns within the same family; teachers select the examples suitable for their learners. Adding older-grade words does not make every family appropriate for independent Grade 1 reading.
- Every word has an explicit morpheme sequence, its correct final spelling and a short original meaning. New words are not produced by accepting arbitrary concatenations or by importing the older Match-Up bank.
- Spelling feedback distinguishes final-e changes, y → i, f → v, -able → -ability and -ible → -ibility. It does not mislabel the latter two as simply dropping an e.
- **-ous is not inherently a decoy.** The stream excludes a suffix from its decoy pool wherever that suffix can make a supported word. Thus danger + -ous earns points, while a checked nonword match can lose points.
- The spelling section also gives moose–moose and person–people. These do not use a regular added written prefix/suffix and are not forced into this game's floating-affix mechanism. Compounds, possessives, all named affixes, and historical/Indigenous language research are also outside this focused expansion.
- Definitions concern the intended member of the word family. Box uses the container sense and related packing action; the separate sporting sense of boxer is not included merely because the letters fit.

## Verification

The model checks every word's reachability, every incoming wave's useful choices, new-word-only scoring, completed branches, and the new curriculum example chains. Browser checks cover the original game mechanics plus the named pony/leaf examples, dangerous as a positive match, -ability/-ibility, -en/-ize, long words on a phone, enlarged family selection and an actual offline copy.
