#ForYou: Rules Summary

Goal: Figure out the hidden algorithm VidYou is using and play cards that match it to score points!

  • Round 1 (Popularity): VidYou plays 1 Optimization card (visible) and 3 Algorithm cards (hidden, ranked 1st, 2nd, 3rd). Play Video/Boost cards from your hand. Turn 1: Play 1 card. Turn 2: Play up to 2 cards.
  • Round 2 (Monetization): VidYou plays 3 Audience cards (hidden). Play Ad and Data cards to target the audience.
  • Round 3 (Machine Learning): Similar to Round 2, using Ad and Data cards to hit Audience targets.

Scoring: Matching the 1st hidden card = 3pts, 2nd = 2pts, 3rd = 1pt. Boost/Data cards enhance your main cards!

Note: You can stop playing at the end of any round. Points carry over sequentially.
