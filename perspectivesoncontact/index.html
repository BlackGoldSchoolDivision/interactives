Your Journey Overview

Trade & Economy

Initial mutually beneficial exchange of goods (furs for metal/cloth) eventually shifted to economic dependence and depletion of resources for Indigenous peoples.

Population & Health

The introduction of European diseases (like smallpox) caused catastrophic depopulation among Indigenous communities, devastating social structures.

Governance & Land

European imperialism led to the imposition of foreign governance systems, treaties, and policies aimed at assimilation (like Residential Schools), significantly impacting Indigenous sovereignty.
