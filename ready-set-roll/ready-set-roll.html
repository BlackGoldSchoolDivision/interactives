How this model works

There is no motor. Gravity speeds up the cars downhill. Their wheels turn with the distance they roll. Rolling resistance and a small air-resistance term remove motion, so the cars coast to a stop.

Matching cars share the same wheel inertia and release together from rest. The curved foot of each ramp keeps the movement smooth. Material resistance is chosen to make comparisons visible. It is not measured data for every wooden, felt, cork or sandpaper surface.

The cars stay in contact with the track. This is a rolling model, not a simulation of jumps, crashes or every real toy. Try the same investigation with a real ramp and repeat your tests.

Grade 1 Science: investigate direction, pathway and speed, and how movement is influenced by materials and surfaces.
