# Space Math Explorers — illustrated review edition

Original equal sharing and grouping activities created by **Chantelle**. The original spacesharing/ application is unchanged.

New independent version: space-math-explorers/.

## Play
Four short, touch-friendly missions:
- Crystal Sharing: distribute crystals equally between rockets (total ÷ groups)
- Astronaut Groups: make teams of a fixed size (number of groups)
- Moon Rock Count: count pictured rocks
- Galaxy Equations: illustrated addition within 20

Click or tap a supply item and place it into a rocket, or drag an item into the destination. Tap Undo to correct. Check your allocation, see the matching equation, and complete five rounds. Optional spoken prompts. No accounts or personal information.

Image assets are locally saved WebP crops from the approved AI-generated raster concept board, not SVG icons. This is a review edition; update the Learning Lab catalogue only after classroom testing and approval.

To validate mathematical generation: `node --test tests/*.test.mjs`.
