Grade 8 Math: Discovering Exact and Approximate Values

I What is an Irrational Number?

An irrational number is a real number that cannot be expressed as a simple fraction (the quotient of two integers). When written as a decimal, it goes on forever without ever repeating in a pattern.

Key Properties

  • Non-Terminating, Non-Repeating: The decimal expansion never ends and never forms a repeating block (unlike 1/3 = 0.333...).
  • Exact vs. Approximate: Because the decimal is infinite, writing π or √2 gives the exact value. Writing 3.14 or 1.414 is an approximation (a rounded decimal).
  • Filling the Gaps: Irrational numbers fill in the spaces between rational numbers, completing the Real Number line.
