Teacher notes

Sixty original six-word ladders provide vocabulary, spelling and word-meaning practice. Starter uses familiar three-letter words, Explorer four-letter words, and Expert five- and six-letter words with richer vocabulary. Grade bands are suggestions; choose by learner needs.

These are letter ladders. One changed letter does not always represent one changed sound. Guided clues connect spelling with meaning; read completed words aloud and discuss differences in sounds where useful.

The teacher PDF prints the selected six-word clue trail or a 20-ladder pack. Student pages have blank writing boxes and clues. Answer keys are optional and always on separate pages. A PDF does not include a student’s current guesses or completed screen.

Free climb checks an offline classroom word bank derived from Wordnik. It is not an exhaustive dictionary, and its vocabulary includes uncommon words. Some terms are excluded. Word definitions and all clue trails here are original; no book’s worksheets have been copied.

No accounts, microphone, analytics or server-side student records are used. Progress is kept in this tab. Resizing preserves the ladder, typed word and selected difficulty. Spoken clues use the device’s available voices.

Resources: Wordnik’s MIT word list, jsPDF. Full notices are in the project’s third-party credits.
